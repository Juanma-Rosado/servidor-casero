About me

Juan Manuel Rosado Trujillo · Málaga, Spain

My name is Juanma and I'm in the first year of a two-year web development course (Grado Superior en Desarrollo de Aplicaciones Web) at IES Belén.

Before that I studied IT systems and networks at the same school and finished with an average grade of 10 out of 10. During that time I worked at APP Parque del Oeste, fixing computers and helping the people who brought them in, and did my internship at Vivatech, installing and repairing computers in schools around Málaga.

That's where I got used to wanting to know what's under things. So while I learn to code, I also set up the servers where my work runs.

Right now I'm looking for a company for the first-year internship of my course. If that fits you, write to me.
